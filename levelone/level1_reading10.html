Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Marco: Hey Paul! What are you doing?

Paul: I am buying some flowers for my mom.

Marco: Is it her birthday?

Paul: No, her birthday is on December 18th. Tomorrow is Mother's Day!

Marco: Gosh! I didn't remember! Mother's Day is on May 10th, right?

Paul: Right. And when do people celebrate Father's Day?

Marco: Father's Day is in June.

Paul: Oh, my birthday is in June, too. And when is your birthday?

Marco: My birthday is on January 21st.

Paul: Oh, I see. Well, will you buy some flowers for your mom?

Marco: I think so! And I'm going to make a reservation in a restaurant.

Paul: Sounds good. Have a nice day!

Marco: You too!

Conversation 2

Alice: Hey Henry, are you going to go to the surprise party for Jenny this weekend?

Henry: You told me about that yesterday, but I don't know yet. When is it going to be?

Alice: It's going to be on Saturday at 7:00.

Henry: Where is it going to be?

Alice: It's going to take place at my house. Jenny is leaving the city to study abroad for six months.

Henry: So, are you going to throw a farewell party for her?

Alice: Yes, we are, and it is going to be a surprise for her.

Henry: Ok, count on me!

Step 2: Key Vocabulary & Expressions (Paso 2: Vocabulario Clave y Expresiones)

  • Months: January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December
  • Ordinal Numbers: First, Second, Third, Fourth, Fifth, Sixth, Seventh, Eighth, Ninth, Tenth, Eleventh, Twelfth, Thirteenth, Fourteenth, Fifteenth, Sixteenth, Seventeenth, Eighteenth, Nineteenth, Twentieth, Twenty-first, Thirtieth
  • Special Occasions: Anniversary, Birthday, Day of the Dead, Father's Day, Mother's Day, New Year's Eve, Valentine's Day
  • Celebrations and Activities: Buy a card, Buy presents, Eat cake, Get together, Give flowers, Have a barbecue, Light firecrackers, Parade, Surprise party, Throw a party, Trick or treat, Visit relatives, Wear a costume
  • Dates: "on" + date: on December 18th, on May 10th, on January 21st. "in" + month: in June.
  • Future (going to): "I'm going to make a reservation.", "It's going to be on Saturday at 7:00.", "Are you going to throw a farewell party?", "We are going to throw a surprise party."
  • Useful Questions: "When is your birthday?", "Is it her birthday?", "When is it going to be?", "Where is it going to be?", "Are you going to go to the party?"

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. In Conversation 1, what is Paul buying?
  2. In Conversation 1, when is Paul's mom's birthday?
  3. In Conversation 1, when is Marco's birthday?
  4. In Conversation 1, what is Marco going to do?
  5. In Conversation 2, when is the surprise party?
  6. In Conversation 2, why is Jenny leaving the city?
