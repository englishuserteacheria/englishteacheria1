Reading Lesson 1

Step 1: Introduction

Welcome to your first reading lesson. In this lesson, we will focus on basic greetings and introductions.

Step 2: Vocabulary

  • Hello: Hola
  • Good morning: Buenos días
  • Friend: Amigo/a

Step 3: Reading Practice

Hello! Good morning. My name is Alex. I am a student and this is my friend, Sarah. We are learning English today.

Step 4: Exercises

Answer the following questions based on the text:

  1. What is the boy's name?
  2. Who is Sarah?
