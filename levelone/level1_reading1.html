Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Ana Student: Good morning teacher, may I come in?

Teacher Marisol: Sure, come in. What's your name?

Anna: My name is Anna.

Teacher Marisol: Nice to meet you, I'm teacher Marisol. Can you spell your name?

Anna: A – N – N – A.

Conversation 2

Jeffrey: Good evening Abby.

Abby: Hi Jeffrey, how are you?

Jeffrey: I'm fine, thank you. This is Alex, a new student.

Abby: Nice to meet you, Alex.

Alex: Nice to meet you too.

Jeffrey: He wants to book his next appointment.

Abby: Sure! What is your last name?

Alex: Smith.

Abby: How do you spell that?

Alex: S – M – I – T – H.

Conversation 3

Hanna: Can I borrow a pencil, teacher?

Henry: Of course, these are my pencils.

Hanna: Thanks, and what are those?

Henry: They are pens and markers. By the way, this is your book.

Hanna: Oh, what is that?

Henry: It is a dictionary.

Hanna: Cool! Can I borrow it?

Henry: Sure. Is this your eraser?

Hanna: Yes, it is, and that is my sharpener. You can borrow them.

Hanna: Thank you!

Step 2: Key Vocabulary & Expressions (Paso 2: Vocabulario Clave y Expresiones)

  • Greetings: Good morning, Good evening, Hi, Thanks, Thank you
  • Introductions: My name is..., This is...
  • Polite Expressions: Nice to meet you, May I come in?, Sure, You can borrow them
  • Spelling & Questions: "How do you spell that?", "What is that?", "What are those?"
  • School Supplies & Demonstratives: Pencil, Pencils, Pen, Pens, Markers, Book, Dictionary, Eraser, Sharpener, This, These, That, Those

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. What is the new student's name in Conversation 1?
  2. What is Alex's last name?
  3. How do you spell Alex's last name?
  4. In Conversation 3, what objects does the woman ask to borrow?
  5. According to Conversation 3, what item is identified as a dictionary?
