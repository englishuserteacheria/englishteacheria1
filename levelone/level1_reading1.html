Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Anna: Good morning teacher, may I come in?

Teacher Marisol: Sure, come in. What's your name?

Anna: My name is Anna.

Teacher Marisol: Nice to meet you, I'm teacher Marisol. Can you spell your name?

Anna: A – N – N – A.

Conversation 2

Jeffrey: Good evening Abby.

Abby: Hi Jeffrey, how are you?

Jeffrey: I'm fine, thank you. This is Alex, a new student.

Abby: Nice to meet you, Alex.

Alex: Nice to meet you too.

Jeffrey: He wants to book his next appointment.

Abby: Sure! What is your last name?

Alex: Smith.

Abby: How do you spell that?

Alex: S – M – I – T – H.

Conversation 3

Hanna: Can I borrow a pencil, teacher?

Henry: Of course, these are my pencils.

Hanna: Thanks, and what are those?

Henry: They are pens and markers. By the way, this is your book.

Hanna: Oh, what is that?

Henry: It is a dictionary.

Hanna: Cool! Can I borrow it?

Henry: Sure. Is this your eraser?

Hanna: Yes, it is, and that is my sharpener. You can borrow them.

Hanna: Thank you!

Step 2: Key Vocabulary & Expressions (Paso 2: Vocabulario Clave y Expresiones)

  • Greetings: Hello, Hi, Good morning, Good evening, How are you?, Fine thank you, Goodbye
  • Introductions: My name is..., This is..., Nice to meet you
  • Polite Expressions: May I come in?, Sure, Of course, Thanks, Thank you, Can I borrow...?, You can borrow them
  • Spelling & Questions: "What is your name?", "What is your last name?", "How do you spell that?", "What is that?", "What are those?", "Is this your eraser?"
  • School Supplies: Pencil, Pen, Marker, Book, Dictionary, Eraser, Sharpener
  • Demonstratives: This, These, That, Those
  • Verbs: Come in, Listen, Repeat, Spell, Write

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. In Conversation 1, what is the student's name?
  2. In Conversation 2, what is the new student's name?
  3. In Conversation 2, what is Alex's last name?
  4. How do you spell Alex's last name? (Write the letters separated by dashes.)
  5. In Conversation 3, what does Hanna ask to borrow at the beginning?
  6. In Conversation 3, what is the book that Hanna wants to borrow?

Step 4: Speaking & Practice (Paso 4: Expresión Oral y Práctica)

Answer the questions out loud using voice input or type your response below: (Responde a las preguntas en voz alta usando la entrada de voz o escribe tus respuestas abajo:)

Question 1: "What is your name and how do you spell it?"




Question 2: "What school supplies do you have on your desk? (Mention a pen, pencil, or book using this or that)."




Question 3: "Can you ask to borrow a marker or an eraser politely in English?"
