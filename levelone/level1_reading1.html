Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Student: Good morning teacher, may I come in?

Teacher Marisol: Sure, come in. What's your name?

Anna: My name is Anna.

Teacher Marisol: Nice to meet you, I'm teacher Marisol. Can you spell your name?

Anna: A – N – N – A.

Conversation 2

Jeffrey: Good evening Abby.

Abby: Hi Jeffrey, how are you?

Jeffrey: I'm fine, thank you. This is Alex, a new student.

Abby: Nice to meet you, Alex.

Alex: Nice to meet you too.

Jeffrey: He wants to book his next appointment.

Abby: Sure! What is your last name?

Alex: Smith.

Abby: How do you spell that?

Alex: S – M – I – T – H.

Step 2: Key Vocabulary & Expressions (Paso 2: Vocabulario Clave y Expresiones)

  • Greetings: Good morning, Good evening, Hi
  • Introductions: My name is..., This is...
  • Polite Expressions: Nice to meet you, May I come in?, Thank you
  • Spelling: "How do you spell that?"

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. What is the new student's name in Conversation 1?
  2. What is Alex's last name?
  3. How do you spell Alex's last name?

Step 4: Speaking & Practice (Paso 4: Expresión Oral y Práctica)

Question: "What is your name and how do you spell it?"
