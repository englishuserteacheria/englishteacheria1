Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Abby: Hi Angel, is the teacher angry?

Angel: No, he is not. I think he is stressed or tired.

Abby: Oh…

Angel: Are you tired, too?

Abby: No, I am not, but I am a bit nervous because I am having an oral exam.

Angel: Well, good luck with your exam!

Conversation 2

Jeffrey: Hey Anna, are you bored?

Anna: No, I am not…

Jeffrey: Then why the long face? Are you sad?

Anna: No, I am not, I am sick. But, how are you?

Jeffrey: I am happy, it is Friday!

Conversation 3

Joseph: Hello, what is your name?

Marco: I am Marco.

Joseph: Nice to meet you Marco, I am Joseph. Where are you from?

Marco: I am from Italy.

Joseph: Cool! So you are Italian! What city are you from?

Marco: I am from Roma, and where are you from?

Joseph: I am from Toronto.

Marco: Oh, so you are Canadian.

Joseph: Yes, I am. Well, nice to meet you, see you!

Marco: Good bye!

Conversation 4

Joseph: Hey Marco, what is your phone number?

Marco: It is 8 3 3 2 1 4 7 2 0 8.

Joseph: And what is your email address?

Marco: It is marco_92@english-user.com.

Joseph: Ok, thank you.

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. In Conversation 1, why is the woman nervous?
  2. In Conversation 2, what is wrong with Anna?
  3. In Conversation 2, why is the man happy?

  5. In Conversation 3, what city is Joseph from?
