Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Conversation 1

Hanna: Hi Angel, is the teacher angry?

Angel: No, he is not. I think he is stressed or tired.

Hanna: Oh…

Angel: Are you tired, too?

Hanna: No, I am not, but I am a bit nervous because I’m having an oral exam.

Angel: Well, good luck with your exam!

Conversation 2

Alex: Hey Anna, are you bored?

Anna: No, I am not…

Alex: Then why the long face? Are you sad?

Anna: No, I am not, I am sick. But, how are you?

Alex: I am happy, it is Friday!

Conversation 3

Joseph: Hello, what’s your name?

Marco: I’m Marco.

Joseph: Nice to meet you Marco, I’m Joseph. Where are you from?

Marco: I’m from Italy.

Joseph: Cool! So you are Italian! What city are you from?

Marco: I am from Roma, and where are you from?

Joseph: I’m from Toronto.

Marco: Oh, so you’re Canadian.

Joseph: Yes, I am. Well, nice to meet you, see you!

Marco: Good bye!

Conversation 4

Joseph: Hey Marco, what is your phone number?

Marco: It is 8 3 3 2 1 4 7 2 0 8.

Joseph: And what’s your email address?

Marco: It is marco_92@english-user.com.

Joseph: Ok, thank you.
