Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Marco: Hey buddy, are you ready for the trip?

Joseph: Not yet, it's on Friday!

Marco: I know, but it is important to pack your bags in advance.

Joseph: Well you are right, what should I pack?

Marco: Well, it is important to have a map with you to not get lost.

Joseph: What else?

Marco: You need to take sunscreen with you to protect your skin from the sun, and get some snacks too.

Joseph: Why?

Marco: You need snacks because you may feel hungry during the trip.

Joseph: Oh, ok.

Marco: Don't forget your charger and take with you a credit card to have money all the time.

Joseph: I will, thanks for your recommendations.

Marco: You're welcome.

Conversation 2

Abby: Hey Sally, where were you? I was looking for you!

Sally: I was at the library.

Abby: Oh I see, hey, were you at the party last night? I didn't see you!

Sally: I wasn't. How was it?

Abby: It was great, I had so much fun.

Sally: Were your cousins with you?

Abby: Oh no, they weren't, they were at home.

Conversation 3

Jeffrey: Hello Marco, how was your weekend?

Marco: It was kinda boring, I was sick, so I stayed home.

Jeffrey: So bad! But, did you rest?

Marco: Oh yeah, I rested and relaxed, I slept in and watched some movies.

Jeffrey: Well, that doesn't sound bad.

Marco: Did you rest?

Jeffrey: No, I didn't. I also stayed home but I did housework, went grocery shopping and gave my dog a bath on Saturday, so I didn't rest.

Marco: Did you do anything funny on Sunday?

Jeffrey: I did, I had a barbecue and got together with some friends.

Marco: Did you stay up late?

Jeffrey: Yes, I did, I'm a little bit sleepy now!

  • Trips and Travel: Business trip, Camping, Ecotour, Field trip, Get on the bus, Get on the plane, Pack your bags
  • Travel Items: Camera, GPS, Insect repellent, Map, Snacks, Suitcase, Sunscreen, Ticket
  • Opinions: Awesome, Bad, Great, OK, So-so, Terrible
  • Weekend Activities: Do housework, Drive around the city, Get together with friends, Get your nails done, Give your dog a bath, Go grocery shopping, Go to the movies, Go to the river, Go to the spa, Have a barbecue, Have a party, Play a sport, Play board games, Play video games, Read a book, Relax, Rest, Sleep in, Stay home, Stay up late, Visit a theme park, Visit family
  • Past Simple (To Be): I was, you were, it was, they were, I wasn't, they weren't
  • Past Simple (Actions): I stayed home, I rested, I slept in, I did housework, I went grocery shopping, I gave my dog a bath, I had a barbecue, I got together with friends
  • Useful Questions: "Where were you?", "Were you at the party last night?", "How was your weekend?", "Did you rest?", "Did you stay up late?"

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. In Conversation 1, what day is the trip?
  2. In Conversation 1, why does Joseph need snacks?
  3. In Conversation 1, what does Joseph need to protect his skin from the sun?

  5. In Conversation 2, how was the party?
