Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Abby: Hey Marco, can I ask you something?

Marco: Sure, what’s up?

Abby: I heard your mom is a nurse…

Marco: Yes, she is. She works in a hospital.

Abby: And what does she do?

Marco: She helps doctors take care of patients…

Abby: Does your dad work in a hospital too?

Marco: No, he doesn’t. He is a musician.

Abby: Oh, what does he do?

Marco: He plays the guitar, the piano and the violin.

Abby: What company does he work for?

Marco: He works for a symphony orchestra in the city.

Abby: Cool! Can you play an instrument?

Marco: Oh no, I can’t. But I can sing. Can you?

Abby: I can’t sing nor play, but I can cook delicious dishes, I’d like to be a chef.

Marco: Great! I want to be an architect, the good thing is I can draw very well.

Conversation 2

Abby: Honey, did you bring the shopping list?

Marco: Gosh! I thought you had brought it.

Abby: Well, don’t worry, we can remember what we need.

Marco: Is there any milk in the house?

Abby: No, there isn’t any milk, neither juice.

Marco: Ok, are there any bananas?

Abby: Yes, there are some bananas in the kitchen but there aren’t any apples.

Marco: Is there any meat in the fridge?

Abby: No, there isn’t any meat, there is some chicken though.

Marco: Are there any vegetables in the fridge?

Abby: Yes, there are some onions and potatoes, but there aren’t any tomatoes.

Marco: There isn’t any bread, so we need to buy it.

Abby: There is some rice and pasta but there aren’t any beans…

Marco: We have too many things to buy! Let’s hurry up.

Step 2: Key Vocabulary & Expressions (Paso 2: Vocabulario Clave y Expresiones)

  • Actions and Verbs: Build, Cook, Draw, Drive, Fish, Fix, Help, Play, Run, Sing, Swim, Type
  • Food: Apples, Bananas, Beans, Bread, Cereal, Chicken, Dairy, Eggs, Fruit, Juice, Meat, Milk, Onion, Oranges, Pasta, Potatoes, Rice, Soda, Tomatoes, Vegetables, Water, Yoghurt
  • Present Simple (What does he do?): What does she do? / She helps doctors take care of patients. / Does your dad work in a hospital too? / No, he doesn’t. / He plays the guitar, the piano and the violin. / He works for a symphony orchestra.
  • Can / Can’t (ability): Can you play an instrument? / Oh no, I can’t. / I can sing. / I can cook delicious dishes. / I can draw very well.
  • There is / There are (any, some): Is there any milk in the house? / No, there isn’t any milk. / Are there any bananas? / Yes, there are some bananas. / There aren’t any apples. / There is some rice and pasta.
  • Wishes and Jobs: I’d like to be a chef. / I want to be an architect. / She is a nurse. / He is a musician.
  • Useful Questions: "Can I ask you something?", "What does she do?", "What company does he work for?", "Can you play an instrument?", "Did you bring the shopping list?", "Is there any milk in the house?", "Are there any bananas?", "Is there any meat in the fridge?"

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. In Conversation 1, where does Marco’s mom work?
  2. In Conversation 1, what is the occupation of Marco’s dad?
  3. In Conversation 1, what does Marco want to be?
  4. In Conversation 2, what fruit is in the kitchen?
  5. In Conversation 2, what meat is there in the fridge?
  6. In Conversation 2, what do they need to buy because there isn’t any?

Step 4: Speaking & Practice (Paso 4: Expresión Oral y Práctica)

Answer the questions out loud using voice input or type your response below: (Responde a las preguntas en voz alta usando la entrada de voz o escribe tus respuestas abajo:)

Question 1: "What does someone in your family do and where does he or she work? (Use: My... is a... He/She works in/for...)"




Question 2: "What can you do and what can’t you do? (Use: I can... I can’t...)"




Question 3: "What food is there in your kitchen and what food isn’t there? (Use: There is some... / There are some... / There isn’t any... / There aren’t any...)"
