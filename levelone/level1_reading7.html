Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Marco: Excuse me, sir, is there a bank near here?

Angel: Yes, there is one near here, three blocks from here.

Marco: How can I get there?

Angel: Walk up one block and turn right at the pharmacy, walk straight two blocks, it is next to the gas station, you can't miss it.

Marco: Ok, thank you so much, have a nice day!

Angel: You too!

Conversation 2

Amanda: Good morning, are there any restaurants around here?

Jeffrey: Well, yes, there are some restaurants pretty near here. There is a café one block from here. There is a fast food restaurant in front of it and there are two restaurants, too. Just walk up one block and turn left.

Amanda: Thank you!

Conversation 3

Sally: Hey Alex! Long time no see!

Alex: Hello! You look different! Are you going to the gym these days?

Sally: Yes, I am. I am going to the gym in the mornings, what are you doing these days?

Alex: Well, I am working for a construction company, what about you? Are you working too?

Sally: No, I am not. I am currently studying at the university, I may get my degree this year.

Alex: Congrats! Are you going to school right now?

Sally: No, I'm not, I usually go to school from Monday to Friday, today I'm going to the supermarket and after that I'm cleaning the house. Are you working today?

Alex: No, I'm not. I never work on weekends, on Saturdays I usually eat out with some friends or go to the movies.

Sally: Oh, so you are going out right now.

Alex: That's right ha-ha-ha I'm getting together with some friends today. By the way, it is starting to rain, how about having a chat over coffee tomorrow?

Sally: I'd love to! Call me later!

Alex: I will, see you!

Step 2: Key Vocabulary & Expressions (Paso 2: Vocabulario Clave y Expresiones)

  • Places: Airport, Bakery, Bank, Barber shop, Beauty salon, Book store, Bus stop, Café, Church, Cinema, Dance club, Food court, Gas station, Hospital, Jewelry store, Library, Mall, Pet store, Pharmacy, Restaurant, School, Stationery shop, Supermarket
  • Directions: Ahead, Cross, Go straight, On the left, On the right, Turn left, Turn right, Walk down, Walk up
  • Activities: Chat with friends, Clean the house, Do gardening, Hang out, Listen to music, Look for something, Make lunch, Rain, Sleep, Talk on the phone, Talk to a friend, Think, Wash the car, Write a letter
  • There is / There are: "There is a café one block from here.", "There are two restaurants."
  • Present Continuous: I am working, I am studying, I am going to the gym, I am cleaning the house, I am not working, it is starting to rain
  • Useful Questions: "Is there a bank near here?", "Are there any restaurants around here?", "How can I get there?", "What are you doing these days?", "Are you working today?"

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. In Conversation 1, how many blocks away is the bank?
  2. In Conversation 1, what is the bank next to?
  3. In Conversation 2, what is in front of the café?
  4. In Conversation 3, what is Alex doing these days?
  5. In Conversation 3, what is Sally doing after going to the supermarket?
  6. In Conversation 3, what does Alex usually do on Saturdays?
