Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Amanda: Hello Sarah, how was your weekend?

Sarah: Good, I went on a road trip and it was really fun.

Amanda: Really? Where did you go?

Sarah: I went to Real de Catorce, a magic town in Mexico.

Amanda: Great! Who did you go with?

Sarah: I went with my husband and some friends.

Amanda: Did you try the local food?

Sarah: Yes, I did. It was delicious!

Amanda: Did you visit landmarks?

Sarah: Yes, I visited some historical sites, we even visited a ghost town! But I didn't visit the museum.

Amanda: That's a pity!

Sarah: I know! But we went sightseeing, despite it being a small town, it's really interesting, we rode horses and we rented a car to visit other places.

Amanda: Did you buy souvenirs?

Sarah: Yes, I did, I bought some, and you know what? The landscapes are amazing!

Amanda: Really? Did you take pictures?

Sarah: Oh yeah, I took a lot! Do you wanna see them?

Amanda: Sure!

Conversation 2

Joseph: Good morning, may I help you, sir?

Angel: I'm just looking, thanks… Well, I am looking for a gift for my nephew, do you have any suggestions?

Joseph: Well, if your nephew likes gadgets, these tablets just got here last weekend.

Angel: They are really nice, how much are they?

Joseph: They are 375 dollars.

Angel: Oh, ok.

Joseph: But, if you buy it this week, we will give you a sleeve for it for free. This sleeve is 10 dollars.

Angel: Sounds good, do you have any game consoles?

Joseph: Sure, we have this game console on sale. It is 250 dollars.

Angel: I think I will buy the tablet.

Joseph: Excellent, I will give you the tablet, the sleeve and these headphones as an extra gift. It will be 375 dollars. Will you pay in cash?

Angel: No, do you take credit cards?

Joseph: Sure!

Step 2: Key Vocabulary & Expressions (Paso 2: Vocabulario Clave y Expresiones)

  • Vacation Activities: Buy souvenirs, Explore the culture, Go camping, Go sightseeing, Sunbathe, Take pictures, Try the local food, Visit a museum, Visit landmarks
  • Electronics: Cellphone, Charger, Flat screen TV, Game console, Headphones, Laptop, Memory stick, Sleeve
  • Shopping: Cash, Credit card, Local market, On sale, Produce, Save money, Shopping cart
  • Past Simple (Vacation): I went on a road trip, I went to Real de Catorce, I tried the local food, I visited landmarks, I didn't visit the museum, we went sightseeing, I bought souvenirs, I took a lot of pictures
  • Shopping Expressions: "I'm just looking.", "How much are they?", "They are 375 dollars.", "It is on sale.", "Will you pay in cash?", "Do you take credit cards?"
  • Useful Questions: "How was your weekend?", "Where did you go?", "Who did you go with?", "Did you try the local food?", "Did you take pictures?", "May I help you?"

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. In Conversation 1, where did Sarah go?
  2. In Conversation 1, who did Sarah go with?
  3. In Conversation 1, what did Sarah not visit?
  4. In Conversation 2, what is Angel looking for?
  5. In Conversation 2, how much is the tablet?
  6. In Conversation 2, how does Angel pay?

Step 4: Speaking & Practice (Paso 4: Expresión Oral y Práctica)

Answer the questions out loud using voice input or type your response below: (Responde a las preguntas en voz alta usando la entrada de voz o escribe tus respuestas abajo:)
