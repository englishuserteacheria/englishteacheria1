Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Hanna: Hey Sally, would you like to have lunch with me?

Sally: Sure! What is your favorite food?

Hanna: Well, I love all kinds of food! But I guess my favorite food is hamburger.

Sally: I really like hamburgers too! But I love spaghetti and meatballs!

Hanna: And what do you like to have for dinner?

Sally: Well, I try to be healthy so I usually have a veggie salad with grilled chicken for dinner, but for breakfast I'm not that healthy, I usually have pancakes and a chocolate milkshake.

Hanna: You have a sweet tooth! But for breakfast I prefer fried eggs with bacon, although I like ice cream after dinner.

Sally: Oh, I love all kinds of desserts, by the way, talking about food made me feel hungry! Let's go and have lunch now!

Conversation 2

Joseph: Hi Ana, what are you doing?

Ana: I'm reading a book, I read every night before going to bed.

Joseph: Do you ever watch movies?

Ana: Sure, I go to the movies once a week with my boyfriend! But I hardly ever watch TV at home.

Joseph: I get it, I usually go running around the block but I never go to the gym. Do you ever do exercise?

Ana: Well, I walk my dog every day, is that exercise?

Joseph: I think so, do you ever spend time with your family?

Ana: Yes, I do.

Joseph: How often do you spend time with your family?

Ana: Once or twice a week, mostly on weekends. I often try to call them too.

Joseph: That's good. I sometimes go for a drink with my cousins, I guess that's spending time with the family too.

Ana: I bet it is.

Step 2: Key Vocabulary & Expressions (Paso 2: Vocabulario Clave y Expresiones)

  • Meals and Food: Breakfast, Chicken wings, Hamburger, Meal, Pancakes, Pizza, Salad, Scrambled eggs, Soup, Steak, Toast
  • Drinks: Beer, Lemonade, Milkshake, Wine
  • Snacks: French fries, Potato chips, Snack
  • Desserts: Cake, Cheesecake, Ice cream
  • Activities: Go jogging, Go to school, Go to the gym, Go to the movies, Go to the park, Go to work, Hang out with friends, Play a sport, Play video games, Read, Spend time with family
  • Frequency: Every day, Every night, Once a week, Once or twice a week, Often, Usually, Sometimes, Hardly ever, Never
  • Useful Questions: "What is your favorite food?", "What do you like to have for dinner?", "Do you ever watch movies?", "Do you ever do exercise?", "How often do you spend time with your family?"

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. In Conversation 1, what is Hanna's favorite food?
  2. In Conversation 1, what does Sally usually have for breakfast?
  3. In Conversation 1, what does Hanna like to have after dinner?
  4. In Conversation 2, what does Ana do every night before going to bed?
  5. In Conversation 2, how often does Ana go to the movies?
  6. In Conversation 2, who does Joseph sometimes go for a drink with?
