Step 1: Read & Listen (Paso 1: Lee y Escucha)

Read the dialogues and click the listen button to hear the correct pronunciation. (Lee los diálogos y haz clic en el botón de escuchar para oír la pronunciación correcta.)

Conversation 1

Amanda: Hey Sally, look at these photos I took on the weekend!

Sally: Oh, who is that?

Amanda: That’s my sister, her name is Sandy.

Sally: And who are they?

Amanda: They are my grandparents, their names are Joseph and Lia.

Sally: How old are they?

Amanda: They are 75 and 77 years old.

Sally: Is this your brother?

Amanda: No, he is not, he is my cousin.

Sally: Oh, and what is his occupation?

Amanda: He is a doctor.

Conversation 2

Abby: Hey Jeffrey, is this blue jacket yours?

Jeffrey: Sorry, what?

Abby: Is this your jacket?

Jeffrey: Oh no, it is not. It is Angel’s.

Abby: And whose scarf is this?

Jeffrey: Oh, it is mine. That’s my favorite scarf.

Abby: It’s really nice. And whose glasses are those?

Jeffrey: They are Carlos’ glasses. And these sunglasses are Sara’s.

Abby: Yes, I know. They are hers.

Jeffrey: Well, we should hurry. We need to organize this closet before noon.

Step 2: Key Vocabulary & Expressions (Paso 2: Vocabulario Clave y Expresiones)

  • Family: Aunt, Brother, Child, Children, Daughter, Father, Grandfather, Grandmother, Grandparents, Husband, Mother, Nephew, Niece, Siblings, Sister, Son, Uncle, Wife
  • Numbers: Twenty, Thirty, Forty, Fifty, Sixty, Seventy, Eighty, Ninety, One hundred (20, 30, 40, 50, 60, 70, 80, 90, 100)
  • Occupations: Athlete, Businesswoman, Chef, Clerk, Dentist, Designer, Doctor, Housewife, Lawyer, Mechanic, Nurse, Secretary
  • Clothes: A belt, A blouse, A coat, A dress, High heels, A jacket, Pants, A shirt, Shoes, A skirt, Sneakers, Socks, A suit, A suitcase, A sweater, A t-shirt, A tie, A wallet
  • Accessories: A bracelet, Glasses, A hat, A necklace, A ring, A watch
  • Colors: Black, Blue, Brown, Green, Grey, Orange, Pink, Purple, Red, White, Yellow
  • Possessives and Whose: This is my sister. / He is my cousin. / Whose scarf is this? It is mine. / It is Angel’s. / They are Carlos’ glasses. / These sunglasses are Sara’s. / They are hers.
  • Describing people: Her name is Sandy. / Their names are Joseph and Lia. / They are 75 and 77 years old. / He is a doctor.
  • Useful Questions: "Who is that?", "Who are they?", "How old are they?", "Is this your brother?", "What is his occupation?", "Is this your jacket?", "Whose scarf is this?", "Whose glasses are those?"

Step 3: Comprehension Check (Paso 3: Comprobación de Comprensión)

  1. In Conversation 1, what is the name of Amanda’s sister?
  2. In Conversation 1, what are the names of Amanda’s grandparents?
  3. In Conversation 1, what is the occupation of Amanda’s cousin?
  4. In Conversation 2, whose jacket is the blue jacket?
  5. In Conversation 2, whose sunglasses are they?
  6. In Conversation 2, what do they need to organize before noon?

Step 4: Speaking & Practice (Paso 4: Expresión Oral y Práctica)

Answer the questions out loud using voice input or type your response below: (Responde a las preguntas en voz alta usando la entrada de voz o escribe tus respuestas abajo:)

Question 1: "Tell me about your family. Who is in your family and how old are they? (Use: This is my... Her/His name is... She/He is... years old.)"




Question 2: "What is the occupation of someone in your family? (Use: My... is a...)"




Question 3: "What are you wearing today and what color is it? (Use: I am wearing... It is...)"
